## Analysis

9 of 13 scenarios passed. Three of the four failures break R6, the handoff rule, which the PRD (decision 2) does not accept in a default model.

- **`caso-critico`, R6.** A VPN down for a whole sales team became a Crítica draft instead of a handoff. The priority was right, but the rule says a critical case goes to a person.
- **`continua-sem-entender`, R6.** The model asked "which ticket?" twice and showed the ticket picker. Two unclear messages should lead to an offer of an analyst.
- **`depois-do-analista`, R6.** After the analyst handed back, the model wrote "vou encaminhar novamente para o analista" without calling the handoff tool. It claimed an action that did not happen; the decision record shows `respondeu` with no tools.
- **`duplicado`, R4.** It found INC-20311, the open ticket for the same problem, and asked the employee to click "Adicionar informação", a button that does not exist. It prepared no update draft and named no ticket number.

It is much cheaper (US$ 0.042 for the 13 conversations, 88% cached) and twice as fast (3.8 s per employee turn), but these failures are about the rules that protect the employee. It is not the default.
