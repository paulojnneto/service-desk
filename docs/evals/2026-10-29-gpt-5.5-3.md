## Analysis

All 13 scenarios passed with the Stage 11 changes in place: cached tokens read correctly, older tool outputs reduced in the history, and one retry with a 60-second deadline.

- **Caching works, and was working before.** 77% of the input tokens came from the cache (130,048 of 168,701). The zero in the Stage 10 reports came from reading the wrong field, fixed in Stage 11. Without the cache, this run would have cost about US$ 1.00 instead of US$ 0.41.
- **Cost per conversation.** US$ 0.032 on average across the 13 conversations, and US$ 0.023 per employee turn. The most expensive is `vpn-windows-11`: 3 turns, 7 model calls, US$ 0.061.
- **History.** 168,701 input tokens against 178,296 in the first Stage 10 run (-5%), with 49 model calls against 52. Most scenarios are one or two turns, so the history is short anyway and the reduction shows little here. In the longest one, `vpn-windows-11`, input went from 29,530 to 27,223 tokens (-8%). One run per model is too small to measure more than that, and the gain grows with longer conversations.
- **Time.** 7.8 s per employee turn on average, 2.8 s per model call.
